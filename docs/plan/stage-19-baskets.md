# S19 — Baskets: predict, cover and hold a group of PreStocks names

**Goal:** a basket is a named group of PreStocks pre-IPO names bet on together. Three uses of one concept: **Predict** it (a 24/7 60 m Up/Down Window on the basket's index, test money), **Cover** it (insurance on the members you hold), **Hold** it (the S21 desk keeps the basket for you). Approved by the user on 2026-09-22 (plan `~/.claude/plans/quizzical-booping-ocean.md` §§4–7).

- **No program change.** A basket lane is an attested Series like `OPENAI-60m` (D-100): the relay computes the index from ONE same-fetch PreStocks read of every member and signs it with the price-attestor; `policy_rules.rs:26–51` only needs a non-zero feed id, `attested.rs:104–133` checks `price > 0` and byte equality, prints normalise to expo −8 (`1e11` = 1,000 pts).
- **Index:** equal-weight, price-return, base 1,000 at frozen base prices (`packages/core/src/market/baskets.ts`, pinned by a snapshot test). Units are **points**, never dollars. A base change is feed `-v2` + a new policy version.
- **Baskets:** AILABS 920 (OpenAI, Anthropic) · FRONTIER 921 (+ Figure AI, Neuralink) · PREDMKTS 922 (Kalshi, Polymarket) · DEFSPACE 923 (Anduril, SpaceX) · PREALL 924 (all eight).
- **Decision:** D-124. **Branch:** `integration/w1` directly for A1; lane worktree `../agari-wt/s19` on `slice/S19-baskets` for A2–A6, merged back when its gate passes.
- **Ordering hazard (D-102 shape):** A2–A3 must be running in ops before A4 registers a basket Series, or every basket Window voids.
