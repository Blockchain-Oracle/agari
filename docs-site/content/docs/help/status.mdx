<AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> reports the off-chain actors, the price pipelines and indexer freshness. The Pyth valuation-index row also shows whether the venue key may read OpenAI and Anthropic's indices. The entitlement was denied at the 23 September 2026 review; the corresponding valuation lanes stay unlisted while denied.

If a page you're following shows a stale reading, "signed price stale," or a faucet that isn't responding, check Status first. If everything there looks healthy and the problem persists, it's more likely something in the guide is out of date than the app itself.
