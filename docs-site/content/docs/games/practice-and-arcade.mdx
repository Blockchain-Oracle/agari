---
title: "Practice and arcade"
description: "Play real-price prompts or seeded score runs without placing a position."
---

<AppLink href="/games/practice">Practice</AppLink> deals live Agari Windows as swipe cards. Swipe up or down, use arrow keys, or tap a button. After the final swipe it watches the public price feed for a short fixed interval and scores every card at the same moment. The opponent is a labelled coin flip. **The score is not the Window's eventual settlement.** There is no stake, position, payout, chain transaction or rating. If the live Window list or price feed is unavailable, no round can be scored. Out of hours Practice deals from the 24/7 pre-IPO and basket Windows, and says so when none has enough time left for a round.

<AppLink href="/games/line-rider">Line Rider</AppLink> and <AppLink href="/games/candle-hop">Candle Hop</AppLink> are arcade runs. Line Rider asks you to keep a dot on a scrolling line; Candle Hop asks you to pass through candle gaps. A visible seed generates the course. Inputs are recorded and the server replays a completed run before posting its score. A connected wallet is needed to post to a board, and a deployment without the games store can keep the score only on your screen.

Arcade scores are **server checked, not on-chain**, and no money rides on them. These modes do not use `agari-arena`; that program backs [Duel](/games/duel-and-lucky).
