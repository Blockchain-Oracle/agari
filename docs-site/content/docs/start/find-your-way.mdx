Hold opens the [desk studio](/agents/desk), where anyone can draft before connecting a wallet. For the current state of each lane and its prerequisites, see [availability](/help/availability).

## Choose light or dark

Use the sun/moon control in the header. Your choice is saved on this device; the app follows your system preference until you choose one.

## Share the right page

Use the app's own share controls for a specific market. The public How it works, Demo and Pitch pages explain the product. These docs give the task-by-task instructions. Use <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> if a live feature looks unavailable.
