Agari has a native app for Android and iPhone. It is the same product as the website, on the same Solana devnet
Windows, laid out for a phone.

## Android

<img src="/captures/agari-android-qr.svg" alt="QR code that downloads the Agari APK" width="180" height="180" />

Scan the code with your phone's camera, or download the APK directly:
[agari-0.1.1.apk](https://github.com/Blockchain-Oracle/agari/releases/download/android-v0.1.1/agari-0.1.1.apk)
(version 0.1.1, 59.1 MB, for arm64 Android phones, from the [GitHub release](https://github.com/Blockchain-Oracle/agari/releases/tag/android-v0.1.1)).

1. Download the file on your Android phone.
2. Open it, and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
3. Open Agari and connect a wallet, or practise first.

To check the file, compare its SHA-256 with:

```
7a3d4f2711f0a4dbe4aa6f68fecfe5a8f2de5798a548f8acccaea5434d28901f
```

Run `sha256sum agari-0.1.1.apk` on Linux, `shasum -a 256 agari-0.1.1.apk` on a Mac, or
`certutil -hashfile agari-0.1.1.apk SHA256` on Windows.

## iPhone

The first iOS build is available to invited Agari team members through an internal TestFlight email invitation.
Apple is reviewing it for external testing, so the public TestFlight link is not accepting new testers yet.
Until Apple approves it, open [useagari.xyz](https://useagari.xyz) in Safari and add it to your Home Screen.

## Connecting a wallet

| Choice | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Phantom, Solflare | The app opens the wallet to approve, then returns to Agari. |
| Android wallet | Android's Mobile Wallet Adapter lets you choose any installed Solana wallet. |
| Practice wallet | A devnet-only key created on this phone and kept in its secure storage. It never leaves the device and is not restored from a backup onto another phone. |

Set your wallet to Solana devnet first; [Wallet](/start/wallet) explains why and how.

## What is different on a phone

- **A first-run walkthrough.** Four short screens explain a Window, how it settles, the games and test funds.
- **Bottom drawers.** The Up/Down ticket, Add funds and your account rise from the bottom of the screen, and the
  ticket shows its receipt in place.
- **One-tap trading.** Arm tap trading once with a wallet signature, and the app signs calls within your limits from a
  key kept on the phone. [Tap trading](/trading/tap-trading) covers the limits.
- **Notifications.** Turn them on from Activity to hear about fills, results and payouts, with a Live Activity for a
  Window you are in.
- **Pull to refresh** on every page, and full-screen, sideways arcade runs.
