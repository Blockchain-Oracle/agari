<AppLink href="/baskets">Baskets</AppLink> groups the eight supported PreStocks names into five choices. Each basket is an **equal-weight price-return index**: it started at 1,000 points at a frozen set of base prices on 22 September 2026. The app reads all members in one PreStocks catalogue fetch; a missing member means no index print. The points are an index level, **not a dollar price or a token you buy**.

| Basket | Members |
| --- | --- |
| AI Labs (`AILABS`) | OpenAI, Anthropic |
| Frontier AI (`FRONTIER`) | OpenAI, Anthropic, Figure AI, Neuralink |
| Prediction Markets (`PREDMKTS`) | Kalshi, Polymarket |
| Defense & Space (`DEFSPACE`) | Anduril, SpaceX |
| All PreStocks (`PREALL`) | All eight names |

<GuideCapture name="baskets" caption="The public basket cards show the index in points and three actions. The number and Window state can change after this capture, which predates the cards' logo stacks and weekly line." />

Each card keeps the same rows whatever loads or rolls: the members as a stack of logos with their count ("2 companies · equal weight"), the index in points with its recent range and its move this week, and the live Window with its countdown and Up and Down prices in cents. A Window nobody is quoting says **No quotes yet**. Basket Windows trade 24/7, so a card never shows the stock market's session.

## Pick the action you mean

| Action on the card | Where it goes | Money and network |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Predict** | The basket's one-hour Up/Down Window, or its ticker page if none is trading | tUSDC test money on Solana devnet |
| **Cover** | A Down call against a basket when the connected wallet holds at least two of its members; one member uses its own name | tUSDC test money on Solana devnet |
| **Hold** | The [desk studio](/agents/desk) with that basket preselected | Starts as a paper practice desk; a later Go live flow would use real USDC and PreStocks on Solana mainnet |

The basket Window settles on the change in **points** between two signed boundary prints. The venue's price attestor signs an index built from the one catalogue read. That is a venue-attested PreStocks value, not an independent Pyth check. Agari's first five basket Windows printed both boundaries and settled on devnet on 22 September 2026; the [source map](/builders/source-map) points to the implementation and evidence.

If the card says **No Window trading right now**, you can still inspect its members and draft a desk. A Predict or Cover order needs a live Window and a quote.
