Portfolio is where a position's life ends up, whichever way you placed it: an ordinary wallet order, a pre-open call resting until the bell, or a tap-trading fill through your Trading Balance session key.

## Open positions

An open position sits on a live Window. If your order is still resting rather than filled, it shows there too — cancel a resting order any time before the Window locks, and get your escrow back immediately.

## Settled positions

Once a Window closes and settles (or voids), its positions move into a claimable state. A winning position pays out by the settled ratio; a losing position pays nothing; a voided Window returns 0.5 per lot regardless of side, because nobody could tell what actually happened. You can **Claim** while a payout is pending. If it remains unclaimed, the venue's automatic redemption job pays it after a short delay; History then marks it **Paid automatically** with a transaction receipt. Check the row before trying to claim again.

<GuideCapture name="portfolioHistory" caption="The connected History shows settled results, receipt links and a winning position marked Paid automatically. No claim was made during capture." />

## Trading Balance vs. a plain wallet position

A position funded straight from your wallet and a position funded through your **Trading Balance** (the `agari-vault` account tap-trading uses) both show up here, but they behave a little differently: a Trading Balance position's proceeds land back in that balance, not your wallet, until you withdraw. See [Balances](/trading/balances) for what the Trading Balance actually is and [Tap-trading](/trading/tap-trading) for how a session key places orders from it without a wallet popup each time.

## If a claim looks stuck

Check the transaction's own status before assuming something is wrong — a signature can confirm without the intended effect landing exactly when you expect, especially right after a Window closes and its settlement transaction is still confirming. <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> shows whether the settler and price-relay roles are running.
