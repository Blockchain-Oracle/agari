Agari lists Windows in different lanes, because a stock doesn't trade the same way around the clock.

## Regular (NYSE hours)

Nine tickers (TSLA, NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, META, AMZN, GOOGL, QQQ, VOO) each list 5-minute, 15-minute and 60-minute Windows while the market is open, as long as a registered price source covers the Window (see [price sources](/architecture/price-sources)). The app shows the session as a plain word rather than a raw status code: Pre-market, Open, Early close, Halted, After hours, Closed, Weekend or Holiday. The next open or close is named in **your own clock** with the ET time beside it and a countdown, for example "Pre-market · opens 14:30 (09:30 ET), in 4h 04m" for a reader in UTC+1.

## Monday Gap

One Window spans the whole weekend: it opens on Friday's 16:00 ET close print and settles on the following Monday's 09:30 ET open print. This is the lane for a view that only matters over a gap a normal intraday Window can't span — earnings after Friday's close, a weekend news cycle, and so on. It lists Friday afternoon and locks at the open, so a call has to be placed (or rested — see [Pre-open calls](/trading/pre-open-calls)) before Monday's bell.

## 24/7 lanes

Three kinds of one-hour Window trade around the clock, including weekend evenings and market holidays:

| Lane | Names | Price source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tokenized stocks (xStocks) | TSLAx, NVDAx, SPYx, QQQx | Switchboard On-Demand quotes, not the NYSE tape |
| PreStocks | OpenAI (`OPENAI-60m`) | The PreStocks token price, signed by Agari's attestor |
| Baskets | AI Labs, Frontier AI, Prediction Markets, Defense & Space, All PreStocks | An index in points from one PreStocks read, signed by Agari's attestor |

Cards on these lanes carry a **24/7** chip (for example "24/7 · pre-IPO" or "24/7 · basket"). [Baskets](/trading/baskets) explains the index, and [PreStocks and Pyth in Agari](/architecture/prestocks-and-pyth) covers the PreStocks lanes.

## When the stock market is closed

`/markets` never goes blank because the bell hasn't rung. It shows the last price, the last full session's chart, and a strip such as "US stocks · After hours · reopens …" with "Pre-IPO names and baskets trade around the clock." The board then splits in two:

- **24/7 Windows** keep trading, grouped by how soon they close. A Window nobody is quoting says **No quotes yet** rather than waiting on an empty book, and its ticket names the next Window.
- **Stock Windows listed before their bell** sit under **Schedule a call**, one card per company. Pick the Window length, then **Call Up** or **Call Down** opens the pre-open ticket. These cards have no closing countdown and no Yes/No prices, because there is no book to trade against yet. See [Pre-open calls](/trading/pre-open-calls).

A Window whose opening print is more than two minutes late (a paused xStock, or a Window that will void) drops off the board and the pickers instead of waiting there. Its own lifecycle is unchanged: it still settles or voids by the program's rules.

## Windows exist before the bell

Agari's roller prelists the next session's first Window ahead of time rather than waiting for the exact open — a Regular Window can exist hours ahead, and a Gap Window exists from Friday afternoon. That's what makes it possible to place a call before the market opens at all; see [Pre-open calls](/trading/pre-open-calls) for exactly how that works.
