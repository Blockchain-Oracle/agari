---
title: "Pre-open calls"
description: "Rest a call on a Window before it opens for trading; taker orders are refused until the bell."
---

Because Windows are prelisted ahead of time (see [Sessions and lanes](/trading/sessions-and-lanes)), you can place a call before the market — or the Monday Gap, or a Window's own trading start — actually opens. This is a **resting**, post-only call, not an instant trade: nothing fills before the Window's trading start, by design.

## Where to schedule one

While the stock market is closed, the `/markets` board lists each company's next Windows under **Schedule a call**. Choose a Window length, then **Call Up** or **Call Down** to open the pre-open ticket at your price. On <AppLink href="/short">Short</AppLink>, a stock Window that opens later offers **Schedule a Down call** on the same terms.

## How it works

A Window before its trading start is in a **Listed** state. In that state, the venue only accepts **PostOnly** orders — an order that rests on the book rather than taking. A taker-type order (IOC, FOK, or a Normal order that would cross immediately) is refused outright with a `PreOpenTakerRefused` error, so nobody can jump the queue by trading against a resting pre-open call before the bell.

## What happens at the open

Once the Window opens for trading, resting pre-open calls become ordinary resting orders: the venue's own seed maker or any other trader can fill them at their resting price, same as any other order on the book.

- **Default expiry:** a scheduled call expires 90 seconds after the trading start if nothing has filled it — "fills within the first minute after the bell, or your stake returns." This is the default so a stale call from hours earlier doesn't linger unexpectedly.
- **Resting until the lock** is available as an explicit opt-in, if you'd rather leave the call open for the whole Window instead of just the first minute.

## What's guaranteed and what isn't

- Nothing fills before the open boundary — that's enforced by the program, not just the UI.
- No fill is promised. A pre-open call is an offer at your price; it fills only if the book comes to it.
- If the Window voids for any reason, an unfilled pre-open call loses nothing — cancel it any time before it fills, and the escrow (plus the seat bond) comes straight back.
