---
title: "Sessions and lanes"
description: "Regular NYSE-hours Windows, the Monday Gap and the 24/7 token lane."
---

Agari lists Windows in three different lanes, because a stock doesn't trade the same way around the clock.

## Regular (NYSE hours)

Nine tickers (TSLA, NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, META, AMZN, GOOGL, QQQ, VOO) each list 5-minute, 15-minute and 60-minute Windows while the market is open. The app shows the session as a plain word and a countdown rather than a raw status code: pre-market, open (with time left), after hours, holiday, or halted. Outside trading hours, `/markets` still shows the last price, the last full session's chart and when the next Window opens — it never goes blank just because the bell hasn't rung yet.

## Monday Gap

One Window spans the whole weekend: it opens on Friday's 16:00 ET close print and settles on the following Monday's 09:30 ET open print. This is the lane for a view that only matters over a gap a normal intraday Window can't span — earnings after Friday's close, a weekend news cycle, and so on. It lists Friday afternoon and locks at the open, so a call has to be placed (or rested — see [Pre-open calls](/trading/pre-open-calls)) before Monday's bell.

## 24/7 token lane

TSLAx, NVDAx, SPYx and QQQx trade around the clock, priced from Switchboard On-Demand oracle quotes rather than the NYSE tape — this is the lane that's actually open on a weekend evening or a market holiday, for a tokenized-stock holder who wants exposure when the underlying exchange itself is shut.

## Windows exist before the bell

Agari's roller prelists the next session's first Window ahead of time rather than waiting for the exact open — a Regular Window can exist hours ahead, and a Gap Window exists from Friday afternoon. That's what makes it possible to place a call before the market opens at all; see [Pre-open calls](/trading/pre-open-calls) for exactly how that works.
