Masayume's design includes five products built on top of its core market: a supply-side reserve, two specialist ticket types, a financed position, and a separately-funded private mode. Agari's plan carries the same shape forward, but building each of these needs its own Solana program (or an extension of `agari-vault`), and none of that on-chain work is done yet — it's deferred past the 2026-09-18 hackathon deadline in favor of the trading venue itself, sessions and lanes, and tap-trading (`D-084` in the main Agari tree's `docs/plan/decisions.md`).

| Product | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Earn** | A public maker vault: supply capital, the vault quotes both sides of the book, you hold a share of its result. | Not built (`agari-maker`, Stage 8) |
| **Range** | Call Inside or Outside a price band on a Window's close, instead of a plain Up/Down. | Not built (`agari-range`, Stage 10b) |
| **Boost** | A leveraged (2×/3×) version of a plain call, financed by a reserve, with an early knock-out if it moves too far against you. | Not built (`agari-leverage`, Stage 10c) |
| **Parlay** | Combine legs across several Windows into one ticket. | Not built (`agari-parlay`, Stage 10a) |
| **Private** | A separately funded desk with its own balance and slots, kept apart from your main Trading Balance. | Not built (`agari-private`, Stage 10d) |

## What that means today

The app's routes for these exist and render an honest "not live" state — none of them accepts a real order, and none of them is one step away from working; the on-chain program each one needs hasn't been written for Agari yet.

## Where to go instead

[Your first call](/trading/first-trade) and [Tap-trading](/trading/tap-trading) cover what's live today: a plain Up/Down call, on a Window in any of the three [sessions and lanes](/trading/sessions-and-lanes), funded either straight from your wallet or through the Trading Balance vault.
