---
title: "Earn and the maker vault"
description: "Supply test collateral, read your shares and inventory, and withdraw what is idle."
---

<AppLink href="/earn">Earn</AppLink> supplies devnet tUSDC to Agari's market maker. The maker posts both sides of live Window books; its spread and its inventory affect the value of each provider share. This is capital at risk, not a fixed yield. It is separate from your [Trading Balance](/trading/balances) and from the [desk](/agents/desk).

1. Connect a devnet wallet, inspect the vault value, utilization and recent Windows, then enter a tUSDC amount under **Supply the vault**.
2. Review the wallet transaction. The position view shows your shares, share price, realized return and current paper value separately.
3. Use **Withdraw** to take the idle part back. Capital deployed on live Windows stays in the position until those Windows settle; a closed but unsettled Window can be settled before withdrawal.

The Windows table shows quotes resting, paired sets, one-sided inventory and settlement. When new deposits are paused, existing positions continue settling and idle withdrawals remain available. Read the live vault state before signing: deployment evidence does not guarantee that quotes are currently running or that a particular amount is withdrawable.

The backing program is [`agari-maker`](/architecture/programs). The app's [acceptance ledger](https://github.com/Blockchain-Oracle/agari/blob/integration/w1/docs/plan/acceptance.md) records its devnet deploy and follow-up transactions.
