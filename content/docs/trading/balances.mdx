## tUSDC is devnet test collateral

Every stake, ticket and pre-open order on Agari is sized in **tUSDC**: a 6-decimal SPL token minted by a faucet authority with no freeze authority. It has no market value — it exists only so devnet trading has a consistent unit. Get some from **Get test funds** in the header or Portfolio; see [Wallet and devnet funds](/start/wallet) for the exact flow.

### Why a test dollar, and not SOL or Circle's USDC

- **Not SOL.** Stocks are priced in dollars, and a contract pays 1 tUSDC, so a price of 64¢ reads as a 64% chance. A stake held in SOL would also be a position on SOL's own price. SOL only pays network fees.
- **Not Circle's devnet USDC.** Only Circle can mint it, so Agari's faucet could not hand it out. Circle's own public faucet gives 20 USDC every 2 hours per address, behind a captcha (faucet.circle.com, read 2026-09-19). Agari mints tUSDC itself, which is how one signature funds a fresh wallet and how the house maker stays funded to quote every Window.
- **On mainnet.** The venue's collateral mint is a single setting. A mainnet venue would name Circle's USDC there; nothing else about a Window changes.

An ordinary wallet order escrows tUSDC straight from your wallet's token account for the life of the order, and returns it to your wallet (or pays out the settled result there) once the position closes.

## Trading Balance

Trading Balance is a separate pool of tUSDC held in `agari-vault`, Agari's Trading Balance and bounded-grants program, rather than sitting directly in your wallet's token account. Depositing into it is one signature; withdrawing back to your wallet is another. Its purpose is [tap-trading](/trading/tap-trading): once funded and once you've granted a session key, further orders don't need a new wallet popup for every trade.

Nothing about Trading Balance changes how a position settles — the settlement rule (Up if close ≥ open, void 0.5/0.5 if a print never arrived or two sources disagreed) is identical whether the stake came from your wallet directly or from this balance. It only changes where the money sits before and after a trade, and who can sign an order against it (you, or a session key your wallet granted, capped and revocable).

## Depositing and withdrawing

Deposit moves tUSDC from your wallet into the vault account; withdraw moves it back. Withdrawal only pays to a token account you own — nothing here can redirect your funds elsewhere. See [Portfolio](/trading/portfolio) for where a Trading Balance position shows up once it settles.
