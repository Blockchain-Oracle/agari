Agari has two agent paths. <AppLink href="/agents">Agents</AppLink> and <AppLink href="/strategies">Strategies</AppLink> publish or copy devnet Up/Down activity through bounded grants. The [desk](/agents/desk) is a separate PreStocks holding flow: paper practice is available now, and its real-money program is awaiting a Solana mainnet deploy.

| Product | What it can do | Network and money |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sensei | Explains a ticker or position and hands you a call card. It does not place the call. | Read in the devnet app |
| Published strategy / copy | Follows a creator's strategy through the grant the copier signs and may revoke. | Solana devnet, tUSDC test money |
| Desk | Holds a chosen PreStocks basket inside owner-set limits; the runner decides timing. | Paper practice now; a separate mainnet live flow pending deployment |

The strategy and copy path has devnet acceptance rows, but a listed strategy still needs a live runner and a valid grant. Read the app's current state before enabling a copy. The desk's [first-use guide](/agents/desk) and [trust boundary](/architecture/desk) explain its separate permissions. None of these pages is investment advice.
