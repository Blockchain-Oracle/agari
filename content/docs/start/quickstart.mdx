title: "Make your first call"
description: "Go from browsing to a settled result with devnet test funds."
---

A call is a prediction about a listed stock's price during a **Window**: one asset, one open, one close, one cadence (5m, 15m or 60m during NYSE hours; longer for the Monday Gap and the 24/7 token lane). Choose Up or Down, pay the amount shown, then wait for the closing print to settle it.

Agari runs on **Solana devnet**. Trading collateral is **tUSDC**, a devnet test token with no market value. You can browse <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> and read every price without a wallet.

<GuideCapture name="markets" caption="TSLA is shown after hours with the next Window listed. Prices and countdowns change; this capture does not show a placed call." />

<Steps>
<Step>
### Connect your wallet

Open <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> and choose **Connect**. Agari uses the Wallet Standard, so Phantom, Solflare and Backpack all work the same way — approve the connection in your wallet. If the app shows the wrong network, switch to **Solana devnet**.

Connecting lets the app see your address. It does not place an order.
</Step>
<Step>
### Get devnet funds

Open **Get test funds** in the header or Portfolio. The faucet mints devnet SOL (for network fees) and tUSDC (for trading) to your wallet in one flow — the app's own faucet route posted both a SOL top-up and a 10,000-tUSDC mint in a single confirmed devnet transaction during testing, with no separate external faucet needed. [Wallet setup](/start/wallet) covers what to do if a claim is rate-limited.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose a Window and a side

Pick a ticker and a live Window. **Up** wins when the settlement print is at or above the opening print; **Down** wins otherwise (a tie goes to Up — stocks tick to the cent, so exact ties happen more than they would on a continuous price).

Start with an ordinary wallet order at 1× before trying a pre-open call or a longer Window.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review and place

Enter your stake and side. Depending on what's resting on the book, your order fills one of four ways: it trades directly against an opposite order at that order's price, or it mints or burns a complete Yes/No pair against a matching taker on the other side. All four pay out exactly the same way at settlement — the fill path only changes who's on the other side of your trade, never the payout rule.

A **Normal** order rests if it doesn't fully fill; **IOC** and **FOK** never rest; **PostOnly** never takes — useful for a pre-open call, covered in [Pre-open calls](/trading/pre-open-calls).
</Step>
<Step>
### Watch and collect

Open <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink> to watch the Window. When it closes, the venue records a signed price print, verifies it on-chain, and settles the Window — or voids it honestly (0.5/0.5 back) if a print never arrived or two price sources disagreed past the allowed divergence. Either way, claim your result from Portfolio; nothing pays out automatically into your wallet.
</Step>
</Steps>

[Your first call, in full](/trading/first-trade) covers partial fills, resting orders and what a void actually looks like. [What is available](/help/availability) explains which parts of the app need a wallet, a deployed program, or a running service — and which are read-only right now.
