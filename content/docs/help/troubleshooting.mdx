## The app shows a wrong-network state

Your wallet is set to a cluster other than **Solana devnet**. Switch it to devnet in the wallet extension, not in the app.

## The faucet claim didn't land

Devnet faucets are rate-limited per wallet (about once per rolling 24 hours for SOL) and share a small daily allocation. If the app's own faucet is exhausted, use `faucet.solana.com` for devnet SOL directly, then retry the tUSDC mint. Check <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> for the faucet role's health before assuming it's broken.

## A transaction's outcome is unclear

A wallet popup confirming a signature is not the same as a confirmed fill. Check the transaction on a devnet explorer, or wait for the app's own result in Portfolio, before retrying — retrying an order that actually landed can produce a second, unwanted fill.

## A Window shows "paused: no signed source"

This is expected, not a bug. A Series only lists a Window when a registered price policy version covers both its opening and closing boundary. If none does (for example, after the Pyth trial ends and before a replacement source is added for QQQ/VOO), the lane pauses honestly rather than trading on an unverified price.

## A Window voided instead of settling

Either a required print never arrived within its admission window (`MissingPrint`), or, for TSLA, its two price sources disagreed beyond the allowed threshold (`CrossCheckDivergence`). Either way your stake returns as credit — see [halts and voids](/trading/halts-and-voids). This is not a lost trade.

## A feature seems to be missing entirely

Check [what's available](/help/availability) and <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink>. These routes have been built, but each may need its own live round, liquidity, grant or actor. The mainnet desk remains pending even though paper practice works.
