---
title: "Status"
description: "How to read the app's own service-health page."
---

<AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> is the app's own service-health page: whether the off-chain actors are running, how fresh the price feed is, and whether the indexer is reachable. It's the honest place to check before assuming a stale price or a stuck faucet claim is a bug rather than a temporarily unhealthy service.

If a page you're following shows a stale reading, "signed price stale," or a faucet that isn't responding, check Status first. If everything there looks healthy and the problem persists, it's more likely something in the guide is out of date than the app itself.
